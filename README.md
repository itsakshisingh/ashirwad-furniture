# 🪑 Ashirwad Furnex – Furniture Manufacturing & E-Commerce Platform

**Ashirwad Furnex** is a modern furniture website designed to showcase a wide range of school, office, and institutional furniture products. The platform provides customers with an easy way to explore furniture collections, browse product categories, discover completed projects, and request customized quotations.

The website focuses on providing a professional digital experience for customers, institutions, offices, and bulk furniture buyers.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://www.ashirwadfurnex.com/

## 🎯 Project Objective

The primary objective of Ashirwad Furnex is to establish a professional online presence for furniture manufacturing and product discovery.

The platform helps customers explore furniture solutions for schools, offices, commercial spaces, and educational institutions while making it easier to connect with the company for bulk orders and customized project requirements.
